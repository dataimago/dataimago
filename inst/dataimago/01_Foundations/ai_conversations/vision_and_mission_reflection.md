**dataimago Vision and Mission Document**

---

### **Mission Statement**

**dataimago exists to align artificial intelligence with the enduring human aspiration for emancipation.**

We build performance management systems powered by AI that do not control, coerce, or merely predict—but illuminate the path toward greater human flourishing. By fusing interpretive meaning (hermeneutics) with empirical insight (positivism), we design systems that empower individuals, institutions, and societies to move—dynamically and equitably—toward their fullest potential.

Our work begins in education with sgpFlow, an AI-driven platform to raise learning outcomes through precision-guided interventions and systems-level insight. But education is only the first chapter. The future we envision is one where every domain—healthcare, justice, economy—benefits from flow-based, ethically grounded AI systems that reduce suffering, unlock opportunity, and reflect the values of those they serve.

We do this not merely as a company, but as a public mission: to ensure that AI becomes a tool of transformation rather than domination, and that humanity—not its image—is at the center of the systems we build.

---

## Vision

dataimago envisions a world in which artificial intelligence, informed by a fusion of interpretive understanding and empirical rigor, serves as a force for human emancipation. We aim to establish a new model of performance management across all sectors of society, beginning with education, where dynamic, flow-based analytics and AI-guided intervention systems lead to substantial, equitable gains in individual and collective flourishing.

Rather than surrendering AI to the imperatives of profit, control, or surveillance, dataimago places AI at the ethical center of human development—fulfilling the promise of critical theory by reuniting hermeneutic insight with scientific precision. We believe that AI, if aligned with human-centered values, can identify and enact pathways out of suffering, toward satisfaction, meaning, and liberation.

---

## I. Core Purpose

dataimago exists to instantiate the emancipatory potential of AI. We are committed to building systems that reduce suffering and increase fulfillment, not through coercion or discipline, but through understanding and adaptive design. We exist because AI, if left unaligned, risks becoming a tool of totalitarian control. If dataimago didn’t exist, humanity would have one less defense against such misuse—and one less vision of how AI could instead elevate us all.

---

## II. Ethical Commitment

- **Non-Negotiables**: AI must serve human emancipation. It must promote data sovereignty, support interpretability, and evolve mechanisms to identify and mitigate systems of instrumental domination.

- **Guardrails**: We will employ AI to reflectively monitor the very tools we build, searching for early signs of control or coercion. We do not assume human perfection in design; we embed criticality and reflexivity at every layer.

- **Trade-offs**: Our commitment to transparency, dignity, and individual agency will never be sacrificed for efficiency, scalability, or control.

---

## III. Primary Activities

- Designing conditional, flow-based models of progress.
- Building AI interlocutors and interfaces that make these models usable and transformative.
- Constructing performance management systems for education, civil society, health care, and beyond.
- Partnering with institutions to pilot, scale, and evolve these tools.

Our beneficiaries include every layer of human experience: students, parents, educators, policymakers, civil servants, clinicians—and the systems that serve them.

---

## IV. Stakeholders and Beneficiaries

Every person can benefit from the systems dataimago creates. Through tailored AI interfaces, students are empowered to learn more efficiently; teachers are guided in instruction; principals and superintendents are supported in resource allocation; policymakers are aided in crafting equitable policy. In other domains—health care, criminal justice, poverty alleviation—the same principles apply. AI learns from the system and helps it learn in return.

Voices of those most affected—marginalized communities and frontline workers—are not merely “included,” but centered through participatory AI feedback loops that evolve over time.

---

## V. Domain and Scope

dataimago is not an education company. Education is simply its first expression. We are a systems company committed to aligning AI with human flourishing.

Our generalizable methodology fuses two core insights:
1. AI will be a co-author of history going forward.
2. Flow-based conditional thinking offers the most ethically and analytically powerful model for managing systemic progress.

Our eventual portfolio includes performance systems in civil justice (e.g., through acquisition and integration of Civilytics), health care, environmental sustainability, and beyond.

---

## VI. Theory of Change

Systems resist change not because they are logical, but because they are entrenched. Our theory of change relies on building high-efficacy pilot projects (e.g., sgpFlow in state education systems) that produce undeniable, reproducible benefits.

AI accelerates change by reducing the friction between local insight and systemic adaptation. It compresses the feedback loop between action and impact, allowing for course correction that is both humane and effective.

Emancipation is not one thing—it is a living idea. We will build tools to help communities define it for themselves, and evolve that definition over time.

---

## VII. Public Orientation

dataimago aspires to act as a public good, even if privately structured. We are committed to:
- Radical transparency
- Participatory design and governance
- Models of algorithmic accountability open to democratic review

We will seek public charters, advisory councils, and eventually, distributed stewardship models to make this real.

---

## VIII. Time Horizon and Legacy

**1-Year Goal:** Two states launch sgpFlow. Demonstrated effectiveness begins shifting the conversation in educational accountability.

**5-Year Goal:** sgpFlow operates in nearly all U.S. states, generating unprecedented student growth. Civilytics and healthFlow are live in initial jurisdictions.

**25-Year Vision:** dataimago is the global leader in human-centered performance systems. We have redefined AI as a technology of alignment, empathy, and collective care.

**Legacy:** dataimago will be remembered as the institution that showed the world that AI could be used *not to enslave*, but to *liberate*—not to predict the future, but to help us build one worth living in.
