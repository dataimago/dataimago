# dataimago Brand Guidelines

*Visual identity for emancipatory AI*

---

## Brand Philosophy

dataimago's visual identity must embody the tension at the heart of our mission: **rigorous philosophical thinking made accessible for transformative action**. We bridge academic critical theory with practical AI development, serving audiences from philosophers to policymakers to the general public.

Our visual language should reflect:
- **Intellectual rigor** without elitism
- **Critical consciousness** without pessimism  
- **Technological sophistication** without techno-utopianism
- **Emancipatory vision** without naive optimism

## Brand Personality

### Core Attributes
- **Accessible-yet-Futuristic**: Complex ideas made immediate and forward-looking
- **Critically Direct**: Bold communication that cuts through noise
- **Technologically Sophisticated**: Advanced capabilities presented clearly
- **Emancipatory**: Optimistic about human potential enhanced by AI
- **Dialectical**: Embraces contradiction while driving toward synthesis

### Voice and Tone
- **Bold, declarative statements** that demand attention
- **High-contrast communication** that makes abstract concepts immediate
- **Accessible futurism** that bridges human warmth with technological precision
- **Critical directness** without academic pretension

### Logo Symbolism and Meaning

The name "dataimago" combines:
- **data** (empirical, measurable, positivistic)
- **imago** (image, imagination, potential - from Lacanian psychology)

This fusion reflects our core philosophical commitment to synthesizing hermeneutic and positivistic approaches.

**Visual Opportunities:**
- Typography that bridges technical precision with humanistic warmth
- Symbol/mark that suggests transformation, emergence, or dialectical synthesis
- Color palette that conveys critical consciousness and emancipatory hope

## Color Exploration and Psychology

### Current Red Assessment
The existing red serves as a starting point but should be reconsidered for dataimago's specific mission.

### Alternative Color Directions

**Option A: High-Contrast Monochrome**
- **Primary**: Deep black or charcoal
- **Secondary**: Pure white
- **Accent**: Single bright color (electric blue, vibrant green)
- **Rationale**: Maximum readability, timeless appeal, critical directness

**Option B: Emancipatory Spectrum**
- **Primary**: Deep blue (trust, intelligence, depth)
- **Secondary**: Bright cyan (future, technology, clarity)
- **Accent**: Warm orange (human energy, optimism)
- **Rationale**: Balances technological sophistication with human warmth

**Option C: Critical Consciousness Palette**
- **Primary**: Charcoal gray (serious, sophisticated)
- **Secondary**: Electric green (growth, transformation, AI)
- **Accent**: Warm gold (illumination, insight, value)
- **Rationale**: Suggests critical thinking leading to enlightenment

### Color Psychology Considerations
- **Accessible-yet-futuristic** requires colors that feel both timeless and cutting-edge
- **Multi-audience** needs demand professional credibility across contexts
- **Critical design** benefits from high contrast and bold choices

## Typography System

### Primary Typeface: Futura Italic (Variations)
**Core Characteristics:**
- **Geometric precision** meets **dynamic movement**
- **Technical authority** with **accessible warmth**
- **Forward-leaning energy** suggesting transformation
- **Bold presence** that demands attention

**Variations to Explore:**
- **Futura Italic Bold**: Primary brand/logo use
- **Futura Italic Medium**: Secondary headings
- **Futura Italic Regular**: Supporting text emphasis

### Secondary Typeface: Josefin Sans
**Complementary Characteristics:**
- **Accessible-yet-distinctive** personality
- **Clean, modern geometry** with **human warmth**
- **Excellent readability** across digital and print
- **Multiple weights** for flexible hierarchy

### Typographic Hierarchy
1. **Primary Brand**: Futura Italic Bold (logos, main headings)
2. **Secondary Emphasis**: Futura Italic Medium (subheadings, callouts)
3. **Body Text**: Josefin Sans Regular (readable content)
4. **Supporting Text**: Josefin Sans Light (captions, metadata)
5. **Technical/Code**: Josefin Sans Mono (when available, or system mono)

## Critical Design Approaches

### Design as Critical Practice
Drawing from traditions of critical art and design, dataimago's visual language should:

**Make Abstract Concepts Immediate**
- Use bold, declarative typography to make philosophical ideas visceral
- High-contrast layouts that demand attention and clarity
- Visual metaphors that translate complex theory into intuitive understanding

**Appropriate and Subvert Tech Industry Aesthetics**
- Use clean, tech-forward design language but with critical content
- Maintain professional credibility while challenging dominant narratives
- Balance accessibility with intellectual sophistication

**Create Productive Visual Tension**
- Juxtapose human warmth with technological precision
- Balance serious philosophical content with approachable presentation
- Combine academic rigor with public accessibility

### Practical Applications
- **Headlines as Manifestos**: Bold statements that cut through noise
- **High-Contrast Information Design**: Critical ideas presented with maximum clarity
- **Accessible Complexity**: Sophisticated concepts made immediately graspable
- **Visual Dialectics**: Design that embodies rather than just illustrates our philosophy
