# Governance

## Introduction

As a company whose mission is the emancipation of humanity through AI superalignment, *dataimago* requires a governance structure that goes beyond traditional corporate oversight. Our work touches on cultural transformation, democratic responsibility, and the ethical evolution of artificial intelligence. Governance must therefore be multi-dimensional—combining institutional integrity, participatory design, epistemic plurality, and anticipatory foresight.

Our governance model reflects the belief that **superalignment is not an outcome of internal decision-making alone, but of the systems and communities in which those decisions are embedded.**

---

## Principles

Our governance structure is grounded in the following principles:

1. **Emancipatory Purpose**  
   All governance decisions must be in service of our mission: to use AI to expand freedom, dignity, and flourishing for all.

2. **Plurality of Perspectives**  
   We institutionalize access for a diverse range of cultural, ethical, disciplinary, and geographic voices to shape direction and critique assumptions.

3. **Reflexivity**  
   Governance is never complete. Our structures must be reviewable, revisable, and responsive to new conditions and insights.

4. **Transparency**  
   Decision-making processes—especially those impacting product design, AI deployment, or partnerships—must be publicly documented and justifiable.

5. **Embeddedness**  
   Governance must be culturally aware, socially situated, and structured to include stakeholder voices from communities most affected by AI.

---

## Structure

### 1. **Core Governance Body: The Stewardship Council**

A standing council responsible for strategic direction, fiduciary duty, and ultimate responsibility for mission fidelity.

- Comprised of cross-disciplinary leaders (AI, education, philosophy, law, community development).
- Must maintain at least 50% representation from non-technical domains.
- Charter requires re-ratification every 3 years by supermajority (75%) to ensure continued relevance.

### 2. **Ethics & Alignment Assembly**

An independent, deliberative body that reviews high-impact decisions, model deployments, and organizational risks.

- Includes rotating members from impacted communities, ethicists, historians, educators, and youth representatives.
- Holds veto power over actions deemed fundamentally misaligned with the mission.
- Publicly issues reports and dissenting opinions.

### 3. **Participatory Design Panels**

Ongoing community-engaged groups that inform specific tools and products developed by dataimago.

- Regionally organized to ensure contextual awareness.
- Compensated for time and expertise.
- Reports feed directly into design reviews and iteration loops.

### 4. **AI Systems Audit Team**

A standing internal and external team tasked with:

- Monitoring AI behaviors and outcomes.
- Conducting red-teaming, alignment stress tests, and misuse potential assessments.
- Publishing quarterly transparency reports.

---

## Dynamic Governance Practices

- **Constitutional Protocols**  
  A living document governs how rules are made, revised, and who participates. This evolves with organizational growth.

- **Alignment Drift Detection**  
  Embedded systems of feedback and early-warning indicators for when internal goals or technologies begin to diverge from mission.

- **Narrative Accountability**  
  All major decisions must be accompanied by a narrative justification detailing how they serve emancipation and alignment—reviewable by the public.

---

## Conclusion

Governance at *dataimago* is not a constraint on innovation—it is the condition for meaningful, just, and transformative innovation. In the pursuit of superalignment, we must be governed by the very values we seek to scale.

This document will evolve alongside our tools, partners, and purposes. Governance, like emancipation, is a process—not a product.
