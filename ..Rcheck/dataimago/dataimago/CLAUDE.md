## 🏛️ ABOUT `dataimago`

**dataimago** is an AI-native company founded to realize a deeply ethical, future-oriented vision for how artificial intelligence can support human flourishing.

### 🌟 Vision

To become the most transformative AI organization of the 21st century, aligning superintelligence with human emancipation, and using flow-based performance management to reshape how societies learn, heal, grow, and govern.

### 🎯 Mission

To develop AI systems and analytic tools that reconcile meaning (hermeneutics) and measurement (positivism) in ways that reduce human suffering and enable agency at all levels of society—from individuals to high level policy makers.

### 📍 Initial Instantiation

dataimago's first instantiation is in **education**, through its subsidiary **sgpFlow**. This system models growth and achievement to drive instructional improvement and performance management in public education. Future instantiations include healthcare, criminal justice, and other domains of public good.

## 🧱 PHILOSOPHICAL FOUNDATION

### Core Influences:
- The **Frankfurt School** (Horkheimer, Adorno, Habermas): especially their concern with instrumental reason, domination, and the ethical critique of systems.
- **Deleuze and Foucault**: especially the transition from disciplinary societies to societies of control, and how that power might be reclaimed for emancipatory purposes.
- **Modern alignment discourse in AI**: particularly notions of superalignment, sovereign AI agency, and social accountability in machine reasoning.

### Core Concepts:
- AI should be developed as a force for **emancipation**, not instrumental domination.
- Flow-based, prospective systems should replace static, retrospective metrics in all domains of governance.
- The boundary between "human thought" and "machine intelligence" is dissolving, and we must design systems that honor both subjective meaning and objective truth.
