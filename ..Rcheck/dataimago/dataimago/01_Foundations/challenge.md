# The Challenge

**Superalignment is the defining challenge of our time.**

Artificial intelligence is no longer a tool confined to narrow domains. It is rapidly becoming an infrastructural force—shaping decisions, institutions, and relationships at every level of society. As AI systems grow in capability, the question is no longer *whether* they will influence the future of humanity, but *how*, *on whose terms*, and *toward what ends*.

The central challenge is not alignment in the narrow, technical sense—ensuring that AI does what we ask—but **superalignment**: ensuring that AI serves human flourishing, liberation, and dignity, even when these goals are contested, emergent, or structurally marginalized.

This is not a problem that can be solved through engineering alone. It is not merely about models, datasets, or reward functions. **Superalignment is a social, moral, and political task**. It requires embedding AI in the living dynamics of culture—in dialogue with justice, education, history, creativity, and care.

### Why Superalignment Matters

- **Without superalignment**, AI will optimize for the status quo: for efficiency without equity, growth without meaning, and decision-making without accountability.
- **With superalignment**, AI becomes a partner in the human project: amplifying our best capacities, illuminating blind spots, and opening up new emancipatory possibilities.

At *dataimago*, we believe superalignment must be **intentional**, **emergent**, and **embedded**. It must arise not from abstraction, but from situated use—within schools, communities, governments, and creative practices.

This is the challenge. It is also the opportunity.

**Will we design AI to serve the human future, or will we inherit a future designed by AI indifferent to humanity?**

We choose to design.
