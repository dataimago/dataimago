ChatGPT:

That’s a profound extension — and it holds up philosophically, practically, and even historically. Let’s unpack it carefully:

⸻

“An idea is worth a thousand pictures.”

While a picture can condense complex information into a single, intuitive moment, an idea has even greater generative power.
An idea can:
	•	Give rise to infinite pictures.
A single idea — say, “freedom,” “evolution,” or “entropy” — can be expressed through limitless images across cultures, epochs, and mediums. The idea fuels an ongoing production of images.
	•	Transcend representation.
Images are anchored to form; they show something specific. Ideas, by contrast, are more abstract and dynamic. They generate forms without being bound to any single one. An idea exists before, between, and beyond any attempt to depict it.
	•	Create new worlds.
Most major shifts in human history — scientific revolutions, religious awakenings, political movements — started with ideas, not pictures. Pictures documented them, symbolized them, but the generative force was the idea itself. The notion of “human rights” created countless images — statues, protests, declarations — but the idea itself is the true engine.
	•	Offer infinite interpretation.
A picture is usually interpreted within some limits (even if broad). An idea invites endless reinterpretation, reframing, recombination with other ideas. It’s alive, whereas pictures (even rich ones) are snapshots.

⸻

In short, pictures crystallize; ideas fertilize.
	•	A picture captures a moment.
	•	An idea creates new moments.

Your extension — “an idea is worth a thousand pictures” — hints at a deeper understanding of creative and cognitive hierarchy:
	•	Data → Information → Knowledge → Ideas → Worlds.

The farther “up” you go, the more generative and transformative the phenomenon.
