## Overview

These manifestos establish dataimago's position as a counter-infrastructure to surveillance capitalism and instrumental AI. Drawing from the Frankfurt School's critique of instrumental reason, they articulate how AI can serve human emancipation rather than domination.

### Key Themes

1. **The Palantir Paradox** - How Alex Karp's Frankfurt School education makes Palantir's surveillance architecture a philosophical betrayal

2. **Superalignment Redefined** - Moving beyond preference satisfaction to participatory definition of human flourishing

3. **Critical Theory Praxis** - Implementing Adorno, Horkheimer, and Habermas's insights in AI system design

4. **Emancipatory Technology** - Building AI systems that expand rather than constrain human agency

### Philosophical Framework

Our manifestos are grounded in:
- **Frankfurt School critical theory** and its critique of instrumental reason
- **Contemporary AI alignment discourse** and its limitations
- **Democratic theory** and participatory governance models
- **Flow-based thinking** as an alternative to static optimization

These documents serve both as internal compass for dataimago's development and as public framing of the stakes of AI infrastructure choices.
